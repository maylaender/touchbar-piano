**Touch Bar Piano v3.1** – real songs. Universal (Intel + Apple Silicon), macOS 11+.

- 🎼 All 8 built-in songs are now **complete pieces with both hands**, note for note from verified public-domain editions (Mutopia Project, Breitkopf & Härtel 1878) – no more wrong notes
- 🙌 New toggle **Both hands**: melody + accompaniment, or melody only
- 🎹 New toggle **Follow octave**: the Touch Bar moves along with the melody
- 🎧 Play-along practice: the left hand plays with you, accompaniment shown as grey notes
- 🎛 MIDI import/export keeps melody and accompaniment apart

**Install:** download `Piano.app.zip`, unzip, then in Terminal:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Piano.app
open Piano.app
```
